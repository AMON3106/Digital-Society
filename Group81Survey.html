Scenario

In 2060, EU law requires banks to have an AI agent that restricts people's carbon spending. The initiative began with an international push to help the environment. The invasive nature of its access to people's funds, and the restrictions it poses, violate personal autonomy(Since AI has access to personal finances and has the ability to restrict you from buying stuff raises concerns about violating the personal right to autonomy). CARE — Carbon Allowance Regulation for Emissions, was ratified in Ireland 'for the greater good', fully restricting personal spending in the EU. Now, AI will routinely stop purchases of carbon-emissive products such as petrol, if you have already reached your daily carbon quota. People are also limited in air travel.
